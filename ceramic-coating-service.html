Our Ceramic Coating Work

At Beach Dawgs & Co., Eastern NC's top detailer, we provide high-quality ceramic coating services that enhance your vehicle’s shine while delivering long-term protection against the elements. Our coatings create a durable, hydrophobic barrier, making it easier to maintain a spotless, glossy finish. Plus, our products are low-VOC and environmentally responsible, so you get superior protection without the harsh chemicals.
