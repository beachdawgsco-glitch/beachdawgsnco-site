Our Eco Friendly Coating Options

3-Year Coating

Our 3-year ceramic coating provides a sleek, high-gloss finish with superior water and dirt repellency. EcoCoat Elixir flexes with your paint for long-lasting protection while staying eco-friendly and low in VOCs. Less maintenance, more shine.

​Benefits :

🔷 3 years of durable protection

🔷 Hydrophobic surface repels water, dirt, and grime

🔷 Flexible formula adapts to your paint for long-term durability

🔷 Low-VOC & eco-friendly for a safer application

4-Year Coating

Graphene-infused for extra strength, our 4-year ceramic coating forms a 9H-rated shield against oxidation, UV damage, and contaminants. Arcane enhances gloss while keeping your paint protected with an eco-conscious, low-emission formula.

​Benefits :

🔷 4 years of next-level durability

🔷 Graphene Infinite technology for enhanced strength

🔷 Superior UV & chemical resistance to prevent fading

🔷 Low carbon emissions for an eco-friendly coating

5-Year Coating

Our top-tier, 5-year ceramic coating delivers the highest level of gloss and protection. The Graphene Reinforced EcoCoat Kenzo creates a 10H-rated ultra-thick shield, resisting water spots, fading, and harsh chemicals. Designed with 100% active materials, it’s a sustainable, long-lasting solution for your vehicle.

​Benefits :

🔷 5 years of premium ceramic protection

🔷 10H hardness for maximum defense against damage

🔷 Self-cleaning hydrophobic effect for effortless maintenance

🔷 Eco-friendly, REACH-compliant formula for safer application

10- Year (Lifetime)  Borophene Coating

Our  Borophene Coating offers unmatched durability and long-term protection for your vehicle. With an annual inspection and the latest borophene technology, it creates a super-hard protective layer that shields your car’s paint from contaminants, UV rays, and the elements.

​Benefits :

🔷 Lifetime protection

🔷 Borophene technology for exceptional hardness and resilience

🔷 Outstanding UV & chemical resistance to prevent fading and damage

🔷 Industry-Leading Gloss and Advanced Durability
