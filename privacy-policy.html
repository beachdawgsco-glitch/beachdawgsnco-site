Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026.

Who we are

This website is run by Beach Dawgs & Co. LLC, 16881 US-17, Unit F, Hampstead, NC 28443. Contact us at management@beachdawgsnco.com or 252-933-7311.

Information you send us

The public pages of this website do not collect your information. When you use our contact form, it opens an email from your own email app addressed to us, and we only receive what you choose to send. If you choose to create an optional account, we store your email, name, phone, contact preference and the vehicles you save, in a secured database provided by Supabase. Staff access is limited to authorized people and protected with two-step login.

How we use it

We use your information to answer your questions, quote and schedule your service, and send invoices. We do not sell or rent your information.

Services we use

Online booking is handled by Urable, and invoices and payments are handled by QuickBooks. When you book or pay, those companies collect the details needed to do so under their own privacy policies. Our business email runs on Google Workspace. Links to Instagram, Facebook and TikTok take you to those platforms, which have their own policies.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log basic technical data such as your IP address.

Your choices

If you have an account, you can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete it from your account page. You can also ask us to see, correct or delete the information you have sent us at any time by emailing management@beachdawgsnco.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here with a new date.
