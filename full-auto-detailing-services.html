Our Full Detailing Work

At Beach Dawgs & Co., Eastern NC's top detailer, we provide premium full detailing services that restore and protect your vehicle inside and out. Our meticulous approach ensures every surface is refreshed, delivering a spotless, refined finish. From deep-cleaned interiors to polished exteriors, we go beyond the basics to make your ride look and feel brand new.

Our Process – Done Right, Every Time

We take pride in a meticulous detailing process to achieve a flawless finish:

🔹 Interior Deep Clean – Air blow out, vacuum, steam cleaning and shampoo for a refreshed feel.

🔹 Upholstery & Surface Treatment – Drill-brushed fabrics and detailed and conditioned panels for a like-new feel.

🔹 Exterior Perfection – Hand wash, wheel cleaning, and streak-free drying.

🔹 Machine Applied  Polish and Sealants – Light polishing for added depth and clarity.

🔹 Final Inspection & Touch-Ups – We ensure every detail is flawless before returning your vehicle.
