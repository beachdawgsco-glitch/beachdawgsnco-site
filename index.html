A LITTLE CLARITY

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your next detail.

How do I book, and how do deposits work?+

Book online through our booking page or call/text 252-933-7311. Appointments are first come, first served, and your date is held once your deposit is received. The deposit is 35% of your service and goes toward your total.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Deposits are fully refundable when you cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice are nonrefundable. Read the full policy ↗

What does mobile detailing involve?+

Our detailing experts bring a fully equipped mobile unit to your location, whether it’s a house, apartment, condo, or even the barracks. We provide top-notch detailing services right at your doorstep.

Is there an extra charge for excessive dirt or pet hair?+

Additional charges may apply for excessive dirt or pet hair removal due to the extra time and effort required. If any fees apply, we’ll inform you before we begin.

How long does a mobile detailing take?+

Our services typically range from 1 to 6 hours, depending on the level of detail included in your package and any add-on services that you choose.

Do you detail more than just cars?+

Yes! In addition to cars, trucks, and SUVs we detail motorcycles, RVs, boats, ATVs, and golf carts.

What areas do you service?+

Jacksonville, Sneads Ferry, Wilmington, Carolina Beach, Hampstead, Swansboro, and all surrounding areas in Eastern NC.

Why should I consider ceramic coating?+

Ceramic coating forms a durable, hydrophobic layer that repels water, dirt, and grime, making your vehicle easier to clean. It offers UV protection, enhances gloss, and helps prevent oxidation and minor scratches.

How long does a ceramic coating last?+

Our ceramic coatings last between 3 years and a lifetime, depending on the package and maintenance. The 10-year (lifetime) Borophene coating requires an annual inspection to maintain its warranty.

How do I maintain a ceramic-coated vehicle?+

Use regular hand washing with pH-neutral soap. We offer a free exterior hand wash 30 days after the ceramic coating. Avoid automatic car washes to preserve the coating’s effectiveness and longevity.
